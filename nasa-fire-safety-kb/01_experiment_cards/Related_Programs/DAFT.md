# DAFT / DAFT-2

## Objective and result
DAFT measured background particulate levels aboard the ISS and reduced technical risk for SAME.

## Equipment and limitations
The DAFT instrument suite included the DustTrak and the modified P-Trak. Cabin background measurements cannot establish a lunar dust discrimination threshold.
