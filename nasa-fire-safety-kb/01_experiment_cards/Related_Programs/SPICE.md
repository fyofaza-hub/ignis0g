# SPICE

## Objective and result
SPICE studied the onset of visible soot from gas-jet diffusion flames in coflow in microgravity.

## Equipment and limitations
The setup used a flow duct with integrated nozzles, cameras and a fuel delivery system. Soot onset in a gas flame alone does not determine smoke detector performance.
